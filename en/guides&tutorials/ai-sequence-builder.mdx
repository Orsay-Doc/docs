---
title: "✨ Create a sequence with AI"
sidebarTitle: "✨ Create a sequence with AI"
description: "Generate a first flow, then verify it in the editor before activation"
---

Before you begin, create at least one agent. Depending on the journey you want to build, also connect an Instagram or WhatsApp account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sequences">
    In **Sequences**, click **Create with AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the journey">
    Explain your goal and expected journey, then answer the AI's questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the preview">
    Check the name, trigger, agent, connected account, keywords when relevant, delays, and steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the sequence">
    Confirm creation. The sequence is created **inactive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify before activation">
    Open the sequence in the editor, review each step, and test the journey before activating it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a WhatsApp outbound journey, use the classic editor to build and control each step directly before activation.
